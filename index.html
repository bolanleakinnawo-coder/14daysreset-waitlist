First, let's work on what happens before you speak.

Expressing yourself well isn't only about speaking confidently. It's also about knowing what you want to say, making your message easy to follow and delivering it in a way that helps people understand you.

That's why the training follows three essential steps: Form, Shape and Deliver. Each one addresses a different part of the communication process.

01
FORMMake sense of your thoughts.

Learn to identify your main idea, work through what you want to communicate and find the words to express it.

02
SHAPEMake your message clear.

Learn to organise your ideas, explain yourself in a way people can follow and get to the point without leaving out what matters.

03
DELIVERGet your message across.

Learn to communicate your message with greater confidence and effectiveness, even when you need to think on your feet.

You'll work through these steps during the first seven days, starting with an audit of your current communication habits so you can identify where you need the most improvement.

Here's how we'll work through the 14 days together.

Each stage builds on the one before it. You'll start by understanding your communication, move on to learning practical skills, and then use those skills in your own speaking practice.

But learning how to communicate is only part of the process. To make progress, you also need opportunities to use what you've learned.

WEEK 2 · GUIDED SPEAKING CHALLENGE

One speaking task a day. Seven days of practice.

During the second week, you'll put what you've learned into practice. Each day, you'll receive a specific speaking task with a topic, question or instruction. You'll record your response, reflect on how you communicated and use the experience to improve.

  • Practise with a clear purpose.
  • Work on applying the skills from Week 1.
  • Use your accountability partner to stay consistent.
  • Track your practice and reflect on your progress.

By the end of the 14 days, you'll have worked through the learning process and had seven opportunities to practise expressing yourself deliberately.

A few things you may want to know

Is this a public speaking course?

The program focuses on communication more broadly: forming your thoughts, shaping your message and delivering it. These skills can support everyday conversations as well as professional and public speaking situations.

What if I struggle to speak even when I know what I want to say?

That's one of the reasons to consider the program. The training is designed to help you work on the process of turning your thoughts into clear messages, followed by guided opportunities to practise.

How much practice is involved?

You'll complete one guided speaking task each day during the seven-day challenge. You'll need a phone or device to record your response and time to review your practice.

Do I need an accountability partner?

The program includes an accountability partner to help you stay consistent and reflect on your practice. You'll receive guidance on how to use the partnership.

Will I become an excellent communicator in 14 days?

Communication is a skill that develops with practice over time. These 14 days are an opportunity to build a foundation, learn practical methods and start applying them.

When does the program begin?

The program start date will be announced when registration details are released to the waitlist.
